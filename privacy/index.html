Privacy Policy隐私政策

Shop3D is designed to work without collecting personal information. This page explains exactly what is stored, where it lives, and what leaves your device.

Shop3D 的设计原则是不收集个人信息。本页说明应用到底保存了什么、存放在哪里，以及有哪些内容会离开你的设备。

Effective date: October 28, 2026 · Last updated: October 28, 2026 生效日期：2026 年 10 月 28 日 · 最后更新：2026 年 10 月 28 日

The short version: Shop3D has no accounts, no sign-up and no analytics SDKs. Your likes, favorites and browsing history are stored only on your iPhone. We do not track you, profile you, or sell data.

一句话总结：Shop3D 没有账号、无需注册，也不含统计分析 SDK。 你的点赞、收藏与浏览记录只保存在 iPhone 本地。我们不会对你进行追踪、画像或出售数据。

1. Who we are一、我们是谁

Shop3D (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is an iPhone application for browsing products in 3D. This policy applies to the app and to this website. For any privacy question, contact 361512234@qq.com.

Shop3D（以下简称“本应用”“我们”）是一款在 iPhone 上以三维方式浏览商品的应用。 本政策适用于本应用及本网站。任何隐私相关问题，请联系 361512234@qq.com。

2. Information we do not collect二、我们不收集的信息

The app does not ask for, and we do not receive, any of the following:

本应用不会索取，我们也不会获得以下任何信息：

  • Name, email address, phone number or postal address
  • Account credentials or sign-in information — there are no accounts
  • Contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location data
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA) or cross-app tracking data
  • Payment or financial information — purchases are completed on JD.com, outside the app
  • Device fingerprinting, behavioural profiles or analytics events
  • 姓名、邮箱、电话或邮寄地址
  • 账号或登录信息 —— 本应用没有账号体系
  • 通讯录、照片、相机、麦克风或位置数据
  • 广告标识符（IDFA）或跨应用追踪数据
  • 支付或财务信息 —— 交易在应用之外的京东平台完成
  • 设备指纹、行为画像或统计埋点

3. Information stored on your device三、保存在你设备上的信息

To make the app feel continuous, a few small preferences are saved in the app's local storage (iOS UserDefaults). They never leave your iPhone and are not sent to us or to any third party:

为了让应用保持连贯的体验，少量偏好设置会保存在应用的本地存储（iOS UserDefaults）中。 这些数据不会离开你的 iPhone，也不会发送给我们或任何第三方：

  • Products you liked
  • Products you saved to Favorites
  • Your recent browsing history, used by the “Previous” button
  • The product you were last viewing, so the app reopens where you left off
  • Your language choice (System, 简体中文 or English)
  • 你点过赞的商品
  • 你收藏的商品
  • 最近的浏览历史，用于“上一个”按钮
  • 你最后浏览的商品，便于下次打开时恢复
  • 你的语言选择（跟随系统 / 简体中文 / English）

How to delete it: delete Shop3D from your iPhone and all of the above is removed with it. Un-favoriting an item removes it from Favorites immediately. Because there is no server copy, no deletion request is needed.

如何删除：从 iPhone 上删除 Shop3D，上述数据会一并清除；取消收藏会立即从“收藏”中移除。 由于服务器上没有副本，无需另行提交删除请求。

4. Network activity and third parties四、网络访问与第三方

3D models run locally. The 3D viewer and its models ship inside the app bundle. Rendering happens on your device and sends no data to us.

3D 模型在本地运行。3D 查看器与模型文件随应用一起打包，渲染完全在设备上完成，不会向我们发送数据。

External purchase links. When you tap the buy button, the app opens JD.com in your browser with the product name used as a search term. From that point JD.com and your browser handle the data under their own privacy policies. We do not receive your order details, address or payment information. Purchase links may include a referral identifier so JD.com knows the app referred you; it contains no personal information about you.

外部购买链接。点击购买按钮时，应用会以商品名称作为搜索词，在浏览器中打开 京东。此后数据由京东与你的浏览器按其各自隐私政策处理。 我们不会获得你的订单详情、收货地址或支付信息。购买链接可能包含推广标识，以便京东识别来自本应用的访问，其中不含你的个人信息。

System services. If you have opted in to sharing analytics or crash reports with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may share aggregated crash and usage data with us through App Store Connect. This is controlled by Apple and by your device settings, and is not linked to any account in the app.

系统服务。如果你在 iOS 设置中选择了与应用开发者共享分析或崩溃信息，Apple 可能会通过 App Store Connect 向我们提供聚合后的崩溃与使用数据。该机制由 Apple 与你的设备设置控制，且不与应用内的任何账号关联。

5. No advertising, no tracking, no sale of data五、无广告、无追踪、不出售数据

Shop3D contains no advertising SDKs and no third-party analytics. We do not track you across apps or websites, do not build profiles, and do not sell, rent or share personal data with data brokers. The app does not request App Tracking Transparency permission because it does not track you.

Shop3D 不含广告 SDK，也不含第三方统计工具。我们不会跨应用或跨网站追踪你，不建立用户画像， 也不会向数据中间商出售、出租或共享个人信息。本应用不会申请“App 跟踪透明度”权限，因为它并不追踪你。

6. Email you send us六、你发给我们的邮件

If you contact support, we receive your email address and whatever you write in the message. We use it only to answer your question and keep it in our mailbox no longer than needed for that purpose, then delete it on request. We never add support contacts to marketing lists.

如果你联系技术支持，我们会收到你的邮箱地址和邮件内容。这些信息仅用于回复你的问题， 并在处理完毕后尽快删除（也可随时要求我们删除）。我们不会把支持邮箱加入任何营销名单。

7. Children's privacy七、儿童隐私

Shop3D is a general-audience shopping app and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app collects no personal information at all, no such data can be held. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

Shop3D 是面向大众的购物类应用，并非面向 13 岁以下儿童。我们不会有意收集儿童的个人信息； 由于本应用本身不收集任何个人信息，也就不存在此类数据。如果你认为有儿童通过邮件向我们提供了个人信息， 请联系我们，我们会予以删除。

8. Data retention and security八、数据保留与安全

Locally stored preferences remain until you delete the app. Because no personal data is transmitted to us, there is no server-side database to secure or retain. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to resolve the request.

本地偏好数据会一直保存在设备上，直到你删除应用。由于没有个人信息传输给我们，服务器上不存在需要保存或保护的数据库。 支持邮件仅在解决问题所需的时间内保留。

9. Your rights and choices九、你的权利与选择

You can view and un-favorite anything inside the app, and clear everything by deleting the app. Where laws such as the GDPR or CCPA give you rights to access, correct, delete or port your data, note that we hold no personal data about you; you may still email us with any request and we will respond.

你可以在应用内查看并取消任意收藏，删除应用即可清除全部数据。若 GDPR、CCPA 等法律赋予你访问、更正、删除或转移数据的权利， 请注意我们并不持有你的个人信息；如仍有请求，可发邮件给我们，我们会予以答复。

10. International users十、跨国使用

The app does not transfer personal data across borders because it does not transmit personal data to us. If you email support, your message will be handled wherever our mailbox is hosted; by contacting us you consent to that processing for the purpose of answering you.

由于本应用不会向我们传输个人信息，因此不存在个人数据的跨境传输。若你发送支持邮件， 邮件将在我们邮箱服务所在地区处理；你联系我们即表示同意为答复你而进行该处理。

11. Changes to this policy十一、政策变更

If the app gains features that change how data is handled — for example optional analytics or in-app purchases — this policy will be updated before those features ship, and the “Last updated” date above will change. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.

如果应用新增会影响数据处理方式的功能——例如可选统计或应用内购买——我们会在这些功能上线前更新本政策， 并修改上方的“最后更新”日期。重要变更也会在应用更新说明中注明。

12. Contact十二、联系我们

Questions about this policy or your data: 361512234@qq.com. We aim to reply within 2 business days.

关于本政策或你的数据，请联系 361512234@qq.com， 我们通常会在 2 个工作日内回复。
