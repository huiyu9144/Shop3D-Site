Frequently asked questions常见问题

How do I rotate or zoom a 3D model? 怎么旋转或缩放 3D 模型？

Drag with one finger — up, down, sideways or diagonally — to turn the model. Use two fingers to pinch between 0.5× and 3.4×. Double-tap anywhere on the model to return to the original view. If you let go while moving, the model keeps a little momentum.

单指拖动即可转动模型，上下左右以及斜向都可以。双指捏合可在 0.5×–3.4× 之间缩放。在模型上双击可恢复初始视角；松手时模型会带一点惯性继续转动。

How do I go back to a product I just saw? 怎么回到刚刚看过的商品？

Tap the left-facing arrow under the share button. Shop3D keeps a browsing history, so “Previous” returns to the product you actually viewed, not just the one before it in the catalog. The right-facing arrow opens a new product.

点分享按钮下方的左箭头。应用会记录浏览历史，所以“上一个”返回的是你真正看过的那件商品，而不是列表里的前一件；右箭头则是打开一件新的商品。

Where are my likes and favorites stored? 点赞和收藏保存在哪里？

On your device only. Shop3D has no account system and does not upload your liked products, favorites or browsing history anywhere. They are kept in the app's local storage and survive restarts, but are removed if you delete the app.

只保存在你的设备上。Shop3D 没有账号体系，不会把点赞、收藏或浏览记录上传到任何服务器。这些数据存放在应用的本地存储中，重启后仍在；删除应用后会一并清除。

What happens when I tap the buy button? 点击购买按钮会发生什么？

Shop3D does not sell products or process payments. The buy button opens JD.com in your browser with the product name already searched, and the purchase is completed with JD.com under their terms and privacy policy.

Shop3D 不直接售卖商品，也不处理支付。购买按钮会用商品名称打开京东的搜索页，交易在京东完成，适用京东的条款与隐私政策。

A model is blank, black, or keeps loading. 模型显示空白、全黑或一直加载。

Models are rendered on the device with WebGL, so a slow or interrupted load can leave the canvas blank. Try these in order:

  • Swipe to another product and come back.
  • Force-quit the app (swipe up from the bottom, then swipe the app away) and reopen it.
  • Restart the iPhone.

If one specific product fails every time, please email us the product name — that helps us fix it.

模型是在设备上用 WebGL 实时渲染的，加载被中断时画面可能为空白。可以按顺序尝试：

  • 滑到别的商品再滑回来。
  • 上滑关闭应用后重新打开。
  • 重启 iPhone。

如果某个商品每次都加载失败，请把商品名称发邮件告诉我们，方便我们定位修复。

How do I clear all my saved data? 怎么清除所有本地数据？

Deleting Shop3D from your iPhone removes all locally stored likes, favorites and browsing history. There is no server-side copy to delete. You can also un-favorite items one by one in the Favorites tab.

从 iPhone 上删除 Shop3D 会一并清除所有本地点赞、收藏和浏览记录，服务器上没有副本需要删除。你也可以在“收藏”页逐条取消收藏。

How do I change the language? 怎么切换界面语言？

Open Settings inside the app and choose System, 简体中文 or English. “System” follows your iPhone language and falls back to English for non-Chinese devices.

在应用内打开“设置”，可以选择“跟随系统”“简体中文”或“English”。“跟随系统”会使用 iPhone 的语言，非中文系统默认显示英文。

Which devices are supported? 支持哪些设备？

Shop3D requires an iPhone running iOS 26 or later. It is built for phones and does not include an iPad layout yet.

Shop3D 需要运行 iOS 26 或更高版本的 iPhone。目前针对手机设计，尚未提供 iPad 布局。

The product information looks unusual — are prices real? 商品信息看起来不太常规，价格是真实的吗？

The catalog is a curated demo set used to show the 3D experience, and product data may be illustrative. Prices, availability and specifications shown inside the app are for reference only — the listing on JD.com is what applies to any purchase.

应用内的商品库是为了展示 3D 体验而整理的示例集合，商品数据可能仅作示意。应用内显示的价格、库存与参数仅供参考，实际购买以京东页面上的信息为准。
