Shopping,
in three dimensions. 把购物
搬进三维空间。

Shop3D turns product browsing into a feed. Swipe vertically through one product at a time, spin every model with a single finger, pinch to zoom, and save what you love — all on a full-screen stage.

Shop3D 把商品浏览变成一条信息流。上下滑动逐个查看商品，单指任意角度旋转模型，双指缩放细节，随手收藏喜欢的东西，全程沉浸在全屏舞台里。

See what it does看看它能做什么 Get support获取支持

Built for looking closer 为“看得更细”而设计

Every model is rendered in real time, so you can inspect it from any angle before you decide. 所有模型均实时渲染，下单前你可以从任意角度仔细端详。

Real-time 3D实时 3D

Procedurally generated models with tuned lighting and materials — no flat photos standing in for the product. 程序化生成的三维模型，配合调校过的灯光与材质，不再用平面照片代替商品。

Swipe the feed滑动式浏览

One product per screen. Swipe up or down to move on, tap back to return to exactly what you were viewing. 一屏一件商品，上下滑动切换；“上一个”会准确回到你刚才看的商品。

Spin & zoom旋转与缩放

Drag in any direction, pinch from 0.5× to 3.4×, double-tap to reset. Releasing the model keeps its momentum. 任意方向拖动，双指 0.5×–3.4× 缩放，双击复位；松手后带惯性继续转动。

Catalog & search全部商品与搜索

Browse by category or search by name, brand or feature. Tap any card to jump straight into the 3D view. 按分类浏览，或按名称、品牌、卖点搜索；点卡片直接进入 3D 查看。

Likes & favorites点赞与收藏

Keep a short list of the products you are deciding between. Saved items stay on your device and open in 3D again anytime. 把还在纠结的商品收进一份清单。收藏保存在本机，随时点开重新看 3D。

Buy when ready看好了再买

The buy button opens JD.com with the product name, so you complete the purchase in the store you already trust. 购买按钮会带着商品名称跳转京东，在你熟悉的平台完成下单。

A closer look 看一眼界面

Dark, glassy and quiet — the interface stays out of the way of the product. 暗色、玻璃质感、克制安静 —— 界面把自己让给商品本身。

Requirements运行要求

iPhone running iOS 26 or later. English and Simplified Chinese interface. 需要运行 iOS 26 或更高版本的 iPhone，界面支持英文与简体中文。

Privacy by default默认保护隐私

No account, no sign-in, no analytics SDKs. Your favorites and history stay on your device. 无需账号与登录，也没有统计 SDK。收藏与浏览记录只保存在你的设备上。
